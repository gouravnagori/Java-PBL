The problem / 02

Most people sign away rights by accident.

Contracts can hide important consequences behind dense wording. You shouldn't need a law degree just to understand what you're agreeing to.

THE FINE PRINT ISN'T FINE IF YOU CAN'T UNDERSTAND IT.
01

Lock-ins & penalties

Clauses that tie you to rent or employment for longer than expected, with steep exit costs or unclear notice periods.

02

One-sided exits

Termination terms that give one party broad freedom to cancel while leaving the other party exposed to penalties.

03

Advice shouldn't be a mystery fee

Professional legal help matters. For an initial read of a short agreement, understand the issues and questions before deciding what expert help you need.

In practice / 05

Same clause.
Different clarity.

A sample rental clause, rewritten to show the difference between legal phrasing and a plain-language explanation.

01 / Contract languageBefore

“The Licensor reserves the unilateral, unfettered discretion to forthwith terminate the License without cause, whereupon all advance security monies deposited hereunder shall be irrevocably forfeited in liquidated damages.”

WHAT MAKES THIS HARD TO READ? DISCRETION, TERMINATION, FORFEITURE.
02 / Plain-language readingAfter
! High attention / review before signing

What it could mean

The owner may be claiming the right to end the agreement without giving a reason and keep your entire deposit. Whether that term is enforceable depends on the full agreement and applicable law.

सरल हिंदी में: मालिक यह अधिकार मांग रहा है कि वह बिना कारण बताए समझौता खत्म कर सके और आपका पूरा डिपॉजिट रख सके। इस शर्त को समझे बिना स्वीकार न करें; ज़रूरत हो तो वकील से जाँच करवाएँ।

Questions to raiseNext steps
  • 01Ask for a mandatory written notice period, such as 30 days, if appropriate.
  • 02Clarify when and how the security deposit will be returned.
  • 03Ask to remove automatic forfeiture where there is no stated basis.

Data matters / 07

Your private life isn't product content.

Legal documents can contain addresses, salary details, signatures, and financial information. Privacy must be implemented in the product—not just promised on a landing page.

SECURITY IS A SYSTEM PROPERTY.
VERIFY THE IMPLEMENTATION BEFORE CLAIMING COMPLIANCE.
01

Encrypted in transit

Use HTTPS/TLS for file transfers and apply appropriate access controls and storage protection on the server.

02

No selling. No ad targeting.

Set clear data-use rules. Documents should not be shared or used for advertising without a legitimate, disclosed basis and user consent where required.

03

Deletion with a purpose

Provide a deletion flow and ensure it covers active storage, derived files, and retention limits, subject to legal obligations.
