The Hidden Trap

Most People Sign Away Their Rights Without Knowing It.

Legal contracts are intentionally drafted in dense jargon to favor corporate issuers. Without an attorney by your side, critical traps go unnoticed.

🔒

Unfair Lock-in & Penalties

Hidden clauses lock tenants into arbitrary rent escalations or force employees into punitive service bonds with zero equitable exit options.

⚡

Vague Termination Clauses

One-sided cancellation rights enable employers or vendors to terminate without notice while imposing severe liquidated damages on you.

Expensive Legal Consultation

Spending ₹3,000 to ₹10,000 just to have a standard 3-page agreement reviewed by a certified advocate is out of reach for everyday consumers.

Clarity In Action

See the Difference: Legal Jargon vs. LexAdvisor

Compare the original confusing contract text against LexAdvisor's plain English and Hindi translation.

Original Contract Clause

"The Licensor reserves the unilateral, unfettered discretion to forthwith terminate the License without cause, whereupon all advance security monies deposited hereunder shall be irrevocably forfeited in liquidated damages without recourse to judicial redress."

⚠️ Hidden Trap Detected: You lose your entire security deposit without cause and cannot contest it in court.
LexAdvisor Translation & Plan
🇬🇧 Simple English Explanation

"The owner can kick you out at any moment without giving any reason, and they will keep all of your deposit money instead of returning it to you."

🇮🇳 सरल हिंदी में अर्थ

"मालिक बिना कोई कारण बताए आपको कभी भी निकाल सकता है और आपका पूरा सिक्योरिटी डिपॉजिट जब्त कर लेगा। यह शर्त पूरी तरह से एकतरफा और अनुचित है।"

Recommended Next Steps:
  • Require a mandatory 30-day prior written notice period.
  • Demand full deposit refund within 7 working days upon exit.
  • Delete the clause stating that your deposit will be forfeited without cause.

Yes. Our multimodal vision OCR engine accurately reads printed text, scanned PDFs, and camera snapshots of legal stamp papers.

We use natural, everyday conversational Hindi (सरल बोलचाल की हिंदी) rather than confusing statutory courtroom Hindi. Anyone can understand it effortlessly.

Processing typically finishes within 3 to 10 seconds, delivering full bilingual explanations, risk scoring, and next steps immediately.
